# DisasterSense Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title
DisasterSense: Disaster Risk Monitoring and Response Planning

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
Natural disasters and emergency events demand rapid prioritization. Response teams need a structured way to decide which incidents are most urgent.

## Slide 3: Objective
Develop a system that scores disaster risk and recommends action plans for emergencies.

## Slide 4: Solution Overview
- Risk score calculation
- Incident prioritization
- Action-plan generation
- Python-based implementation

## Slide 5: Risk Model
Explain the weighted factors used for scoring:
- severity
- vulnerability
- exposure
- infrastructure impact

## Slide 6: Example Output
Show how incidents are ranked and why one hazard is prioritized over another.

## Slide 7: Benefits
- faster response
- better resource allocation
- structured emergency planning
- easy future extension

## Slide 8: Technical Stack
- Python
- Pytest
- Package-based architecture
- CLI demo

## Slide 9: Future Scope
Add maps, APIs, dashboards, and reporting modules.

## Slide 10: Conclusion
DisasterSense is a practical foundation for a modern disaster-response intelligence system.
